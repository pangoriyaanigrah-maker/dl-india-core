Saved book every upload is kept and reloaded automatically
Division of labour: analysts own weights, targets, theses and entry/exit strategy (holdings file); dealers own executions (trades file); market data — price, market cap, valuation, momentum, quality — is fetched by the app, not typed.
Signal feed is live: a GitHub Actions cron (build_signals.py) hits Yahoo Finance nightly for prices, momentum and fundamentals, computes z-scores across a ~190-name liquid NSE universe spanning large to small cap (a proxy, not the official Nifty 500 cross-section), and writes signals.json. Re-uploading a holdings file here replaces weights/targets/theses only — it does not touch the signal feed.
Saving is automatic. An upload replaces the whole book and is remembered, so it loads again every time you open the dashboard — no button to press, nothing to set up. Point it at book.json as well and the nightly price job sees stocks you add; connect GitHub and the same book follows you to another machine, as commits you can revert.
